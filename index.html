01
Research

From learning models to
evolving intelligence.

My research asks a broader question: how can AI systems continuously acquire knowledge, improve their strategies, and become better scientific problem solvers? This connects several lines of work, from graph and spatiotemporal learning to large language models, self-improving agents, and AI for science.

01

Large Language Models & Agents

Reasoning, tool use, memory, verification, and agent architectures that enable LLMs to solve complex, open-ended tasks.

02

Recursive Self-Improvement

How AI systems can improve not only model parameters, but also their knowledge, strategies, goals, and execution harnesses through experience.

03

AI for Science

AI systems for scientific discovery, with a particular interest in molecular and protein design and experiment-guided optimization.

04

Graph & Molecular Learning

Representation learning for graphs, knowledge graphs, molecules, and structured scientific data, including connections to foundation models.

AI FOR SCIENCE · KNOWLEDGE EVOLUTION

KnowEvo

Knowledge Evolution for Protein Optimization

A framework for evolving explicit scientific expertise rather than relying only on model-parameter updates. The system represents design mechanisms as modular, revisable expertise and uses experimental evidence to refine them over time.

LLM AgentsKnowledge EvolutionProtein Optimization
MOLECULAR AI

Graph-to-Token & Molecular Representation Learning

Connecting structured molecular representations with language models, including graph tokens and substructure-aware representations that bridge molecular structure and the vocabulary space of LLMs.

Graph MLMolecular AILLMs
SELF-IMPROVING AI

Recursive Self-Improving Agents

Studying how agents can recursively improve their own problem-solving process, including strategy search, goal-level exploration, external memory, evaluation, and harness evolution.
